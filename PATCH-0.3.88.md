# Living Galaxy 0.3.88 — ARIA Keeps the Loop

- ARIA no longer shuttles between a station and the belt without working. A hostile contact nearby is not, on its own, a reason to leave: with a whole hull she keeps cutting with the guns on CASTLE.
- "Avoid hostiles" now means what a pilot means by it. With contacts close and the hull under your repair line she breaks off for a yard she can reach and pay, patches, and goes back to the job she left.
- A job broken off for the yard keeps its contract. Board work no longer piles up half-flown until the desk refuses her.
- ARIA hires, registers a company, banks to the treasury, settles crew and takes armed work again without being told. ARIA CORE is where you withhold any of it.
- The credit reserve guards investments: refits, a charter, settling crew. Repairs are never held back, and cargo and hiring keep 800 cr aboard.
- The autopilot no longer stalls lining up a jump after the target lock breaks. This fixes mining loops you start yourself as well.
- ARIA flies round a world that is across her lane instead of abandoning the mission.
- In the terminal player she sheds deck gravity, floods and sentry when the battery runs down, and waits out a flat battery instead of taking and dropping contracts.
- Wreck and pod recoveries switch the salvage system on over the site.
- The ARIA CORE screen redraws only when something changes.

No Sol reset or save migration is required. ARIA CORE authority ticks saved before this release return to granted once; orders and learning are kept.

This release includes ARIA CORE (NAV > ARIA CORE): standing orders, authority, what she is doing and why.

Verified headless and in headless Chromium at phone size; not flown on a phone. A break-off at the default repair line can be late for a hull with a flat battery leaving a belt under fire — raise "Repair below hull fraction" in ARIA CORE if she cuts it fine. ARIA does not fly the salvage rig. Salvage readiness is unchanged: `aria`, `bench` and `smoke` still open.
