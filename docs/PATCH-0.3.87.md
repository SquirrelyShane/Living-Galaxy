# Living Galaxy 0.3.87 — Dead Hulls: The Rig

- Adds the salvage rig, Salvage's own tool: it works a hulk in reach, section by section, bridge last.
- CUT is fast and takes plate only; the parts and the flight recorder in that section are lost, and half its cargo.
- STRIP is slow and draws more power; plate, parts, cargo and the recorder all come out whole.
- What the rig frees drifts beside the hulk as salvage until the salvage tractor (SALVG) reels it in.
- The quick CUT switch runs the rig when a hulk is locked or in reach, and the mining laser otherwise. U cycles the rig; CON > SHIP > SYSTEMS has the mode buttons.
- The rig and the mining laser never run together. The rig is its own line on the power bus and sheds right after the laser.
- Cutting trains Salvage and Hullcraft; recovering a recorder trains Law. Salvage pilots are issued the rig on STRIP.
- Salvage-line hulls cut faster by tier. Two rig upgrades at industrial yards add speed and reach. Rig reach is on the trim sheet.
- The mining laser can no longer work chunks the rig has shed.

No Sol reset or save migration is required.

Recovered recorders are counted aboard for this session only; they are not yet a
tradeable good and nothing pays for them. Wreck and pod contracts still use the
0.3.84 recovery rules and are not bound to hulks. ARIA does not fly the rig, and
the tutorial does not teach it yet. Income has not been benchmarked against
Mining. Salvage readiness: `verb` earned; `aria`, `bench` and `smoke` still open.
