# Living Galaxy 0.3.84 — Dead Hulls: Recovery

- Starts Salvage career gameplay in the main release sequence; retires separate career version assignments.
- Wreck and cargo-pod contracts now recover finite cargo at their marked site using SALVAGE and operations power.
- Recovery progresses gradually; salvage equipment modifiers increase recovery speed.
- Full holds pause recovery. Return after unloading to collect the remaining cargo within the active session.
- Debris pickups keep uncollected material and charter pay counts only cargo actually loaded.
- Salvage training now comes from recovered material rather than leaving an idle tractor switched on.
- Updates tutorial guidance and contract status text.

No live Sol reset is required. Existing active contracts keep their old behavior;
new recovery jobs use the new rules. Contract persistence across reloads, shared
persistent hulks, physical hull cutting, salvage rights and complete ARIA automation
remain future work. Browser/device smoke and income-parity benchmarks are pending.
