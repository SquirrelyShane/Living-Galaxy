# Living Galaxy 0.3.90 — Dead Hulls: The Loop

Salvage is open: a new pilot can pick it, and it is the second complete career after Mining.

- SALVAGE is a mission step. The autopilot picks the hulk worth most for the time it costs, flies to it, runs the rig down it and reels what comes off, then takes the next one until the hold is full. STRIP, CUT or AUTO.
- SALVAGE LOOP is a preset beside MINE LOOP: cut, dock at the best buyer, sell, charge, again. There is a Best hulk button in NAV and CUT IT on a salvage job in the board.
- A wreck job is a real hulk on the port's approaches, marked when you sign. Plate jobs take hull steel off any hulk. Both count only plate that came off a hulk under your rig; steel bought off a shelf does not fill them.
- A flight recorder pays at any honest port and trains Law.
- ARIA flies the rig, at the conn and in the terminal player. She will not work a hulk with a raider or a drone nest still over it.
- The rig works at two and a half times its old speed in both modes. CUT is still 2.5 times quicker than STRIP and still keeps 85% of the plate.
- Salvage orders are larger and pay more. On the benchmark Salvage now earns 0.87 of what Mining does; it earned 0.65 before the rig existed.
- The salvage tutorial teaches the rig: lock a hulk, cut it, reel it, sell it.
- The autopilot no longer stalls braking toward something just outside a world's pull. This fixes slow approaches for every career, including runs you start yourself.
- ARIA breaks off for the yard earlier when the battery is low, and sheds load while she waits for jump charge under fire instead of sitting there.
- ARIA's learning and standing orders now travel with your account to another device.
- Drone-cull contracts count rogue drones killed in the belt.
- Any multi-step plan you start flies round a world across its lane. A single "go there" still stops and tells you why.
- A hulk you are flying to or cutting is no longer cleared away while you work.

No Sol reset or save migration is required.

Verified headless and in headless Chromium at phone size; not flown on a phone, and not with two players. Hulks are not saved and not shared between players yet, so a wreck job does not survive a reload. There is no ownership or claim on a hulk yet. Salvager drones do not work hulks. With no orders at all, a salvage loop currently out-earns a mine loop.
